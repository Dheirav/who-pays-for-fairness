# 56 — The gap hypothesis dies before its seal, which is the system working

**Individual work, beyond the course submission. Post-hoc, all of it.** The catalog behind
every number is `scratch gap_catalog.py` over committed results; the key sweeps are quoted
below from their stored arms.

## What was proposed, and where it came from

Document 55's failure pattern suggested one variable might unify the week's anomalies: the
size of the gap being corrected. Classic shapes where the constraint has real work; U's,
all-positives and inversions where it does not. The user directed that this be sealed after
the race verdict; the standing rule requires the pre-seal sanity check first, and the check
killed it.

## The check, and both deaths

**Population level.** Natural-point baseline gap against observed shape family, all 31
swept populations: classic family median 0.105, anomalous family median 0.106, ranges
fully overlapping. Texas is the counterexample in one line: its anomalous sex arm has a
natural gap of 0.114 while its emphatically classic race arm has 0.066.

**Arm level** (document 45's original form — anomalies live in small-gap arms). The
anomalous sweeps' arm gaps are healthy throughout: TX-sex (U) runs 0.064–0.114, VA-sex
(all-positive) 0.067–0.128, OH-2022 (inverted) 0.077–0.092 — all above the 0.05 floor at
nearly every arm. Meanwhile TX-race produces +30 to +37 point classic limbs on arms whose
gaps (0.047–0.089) are *smaller* than the sex arm's at matched rates.

**A correction to document 55.** Its sentence "their race gaps are far larger everywhere"
is false at both the natural point and arm level, written from an eyeballed interim
impression; a correction note now sits in that document pointing here. The interim
narrative given in-session repeated the error and is retracted the same way.

## What the same tables show instead, recorded and deliberately not pursued today

Across the six sweeps examined closely, the *slope* of the gap across the window separates
the cases seen so far: classic sweeps (TX-race, VA-race, OH-2018) have gaps that rise with
the selection rate, anomalous ones (TX-sex, VA-sex, NV-2022) have gaps that fall or stay
flat. Six cases, noticed minutes after the data existed, by the person looking for a
pattern — which is document 46's exact origin story. It is recorded here as a raw
observation, it is **not** being sealed today, and the standing rule gains its corollary:
**no hypothesis born from a campaign's data may be sealed against arms measured the same
day.** The proper test set is the IPUMS extract and the task arms, none of which exist yet.

## Where the shape question now honestly stands

Three candidate governing variables; three fates. The base rate ordered one vintage and
failed the next (sealed, document 54). Gap size failed the pre-seal check (this document).
Gap slope is an unexamined six-case observation. The governing variable of the response
curve's shape is **unidentified**, and saying so plainly beats sealing another elegant
story — twice now, the elegant story has been the trap.
